# VITALA

**From Reactive Care to Proactive Health**

### An AI-Powered Personalized Preventive Healthcare Platform

**MEDATHON | Healthcare Digital Transformation | SYHF 2026**

## Overview

VITALA is a Saudi-focused digital preventive healthcare platform designed to transform evidence-based medical guidelines and personal health information into clear, personalized, and actionable preventive care recommendations.

The platform aims to shift healthcare from reactive treatment toward proactive prevention, early detection, and continuous health monitoring.

VITALA supports both individuals and primary healthcare professionals by organizing preventive screening recommendations, explaining their relevance, and facilitating ongoing health follow-up.

## The Problem

Preventive healthcare remains fragmented and often depends on occasional medical visits.

Individuals may struggle to determine:

- Which preventive screenings are appropriate for them.
- When screenings should be completed.
- Why specific tests are recommended.
- Which medical guidelines and sources are reliable.
- How to maintain consistent follow-up.

Healthcare professionals also need efficient tools to review patient-specific risk factors, screening history, and relevant preventive care recommendations.

VITALA addresses these challenges by connecting clinical guidance with personalized health information and continuous monitoring.

## Our Solution

VITALA provides a personalized preventive healthcare experience through five core components.

### 1. Personal Health Profile

Collects relevant information, including:

- Age and sex
- Height, weight, and BMI
- Medical history
- Family medical history
- Chronic conditions and risk factors
- Current medications
- Previous screening results

### 2. Personalized Preventive Screening

VITALA is designed to identify potentially relevant preventive screenings based on individual health profiles.

The screening plan includes:

- Recommended screening tests
- Screening eligibility and due status
- Upcoming and overdue screenings
- Reasons for each recommendation
- Supporting clinical guidelines and references

Saudi national healthcare guidelines are prioritized, with supplementary international guidance considered when appropriate and clinically reviewed.

### 3. Personalized Health Goals

The platform aims to support healthier lifestyles through individualized recommendations related to:

- Physical activity
- Nutrition
- Lifestyle improvements
- Chronic disease monitoring
- Progress toward personal health goals

### 4. Wearable Device Integration

With user permission and compatible devices, VITALA is designed to incorporate wearable health data, including:

- Daily step counts
- Physical activity and active minutes
- Heart rate trends
- Sleep patterns
- Progress toward activity goals

### 5. Continuous Health Follow-Up

VITALA aims to support ongoing preventive care by tracking:

- Screening completion
- Upcoming health requirements
- Chronic disease monitoring
- Personalized health goals
- Relevant health indicators
- Follow-up reminders

The initial condition-specific follow-up pathways focus on diabetes and hypertension.

## Target Users

### Individuals

People seeking a more proactive approach to their health, including individuals who do not regularly attend primary care and people living with chronic conditions.

### Primary Healthcare Professionals

Family physicians and primary-care teams who need a structured, evidence-linked overview of patient-specific preventive healthcare needs.

## Clinical Decision Support

VITALA is designed to support preventive care planning rather than provide independent medical diagnoses.

The proposed recommendation system uses a structured, clinically reviewed guideline library and rules-based eligibility logic.

AI-assisted functionality is intended to explain approved recommendations in accessible language, with traceable references.

Clinical recommendations remain subject to professional review and are not independently generated by AI.

## Clinic Integration and Business Model

The broader VITALA concept includes collaboration with primary care clinics and medical centers.

Planned clinic-related capabilities include:

- Clinic onboarding
- Clinic discovery and appointment booking
- Patient preventive health summaries
- Screening review and follow-up
- Clinic dashboards
- Subscription and billing management

The proposed revenue model is based on clinic subscription fees.

## Running the iOS Prototype

1. Download `Vitala.zip` from the `vitala-ios` branch.
2. Extract the ZIP archive.
3. Open `Vitala.xcodeproj` in Xcode.
4. Select an available iOS Simulator.
5. Build and run the application using `Command + R`.

## Prototype Scope and Development Status

VITALA is currently under development as a healthcare technology prototype.

The iOS implementation demonstrates application interfaces and selected patient healthcare workflows using development data.

The broader platform vision includes evidence-based screening recommendations, AI-assisted explanations, wearable integration, and primary-care support.

These capabilities are part of the proposed development roadmap and should not be interpreted as fully implemented or clinically validated.

Live clinical record integration is outside the initial prototype scope.

## Privacy and Safety

VITALA is not intended to replace healthcare professionals or clinical judgment.

The prototype uses synthetic data for development and demonstration.

Future deployment involving real patient information will require secure authentication, appropriate access controls, data protection measures, and compliance with applicable healthcare regulations.
